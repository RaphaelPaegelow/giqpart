My name is Woohyuck Choi from South Korea (POSTECH), working on Ulrich bundles on Fano threefolds V22 with Prof. K.-S. Lee and K.-D. Park. Our project focuses on finding new description of Ulrich bundles on V22 and their moduli spaces. We also plan to study moduli spaces of Ulrich bundles on Fano threefolds using instanton sheaves. I believe this conference would be an excellent opportunity to learn about the geometry of instating from experts in the field.
