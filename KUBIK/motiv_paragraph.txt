Research : I started my PhD working on moduli spaces of Higgs bundles, specifically counting points over finite fields of certain moduli spaces of Higgs bundles with prescribed residues at singularities to investigate their cohomological properties. Now I am interested in the generalized Springer correspondence, more precisely the invariance of the induction functor under the action of the relative Weyl group. For both of these subjects, I would like to learn more about the mathematical physics involved.
