My research studies moduli of principal G-bundles on singular and nodal curves, with an emphasis on parahoric structures, loop group uniformization, and their relation to geometric representation theory. I am particularly interested in how degenerations and boundary strata encode representation-theoretic data, and in connections to affine Grassmannians and quiver-type constructions. The themes of instantons and quivers intersect with my interests via moduli spaces, stratifications, and their categorical structures. I hope to learn how instanton moduli and quiver varieties inform these perspectives and to connect with experts working at this interface.
