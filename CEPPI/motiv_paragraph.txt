My current research concerns 6d SCFTs on ALE spaces and their partition functions, computed via instanton
counting methods. A key part of my work focuses on the sigma model of BPS strings in these theories, whose
target space is the instanton moduli space described by Kronheimer-Nakajima quivers. My motivation to attend
this school is to deepen my understanding of instantons, their moduli spaces, and quiver varieties, and to apply
this to extend my research, e.g. to toric singularities for which the moduli space is still a quiver variety.
