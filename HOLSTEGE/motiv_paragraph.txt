Currently, we work on constructing resolutions of singularities for certain moduli spaces of representations of quivers (quiver moduli). We do this by translating Seshadri’s construction of a resolution for the moduli space of rank 2 and degree 0 vector bundles on a curve to the world of quiver moduli. Up until now, I have mostly focused on vector bundles on curves. My motivation for attending the workshop is to also learn about moduli spaces of different kinds of vector bundles and how they relate to quivers.
