My research focuses on two main areas. The first are the geometric properties of the nilpotent cone in a semisimple Lie algebra, an a priori simple object with countless hidden and complicated properties, in particular links to Nakajima quiver varieties via the SSpringer resolution. The second concerns SU(2) instantons on S^4 and the ADHM construction. I believe that GIQ 2026 is by far the most fitting event to my research I have encountered in my PhD so far, and I look forward to meeting researchers of all career stages whose interests are aligned with mine.
