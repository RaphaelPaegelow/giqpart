I am Sagnik Das, a PhD student in the Department of Mathematics, University of Kansas working under the guidance of Prof. Yunfeng Jiang. My area of research is enumerative geometry, broadly Gromov-Witten theory, Donaldson-Thomas theory, Vafa-Witten theory and Mirror symmetry.  I am  currently working on some problems related to Joyce's wall-crossing formulas, modularity properties of generating series of enumerative invariants following Arguz-Bousseau's papers "Quivers, Flow Trees and Log Curves" and "Quivers and Curves in Higher Dimensions". Attending this workshop not only will give me immense exposure to these theories  related to the questions I am trying to solve, but also give me an opportunity to discuss with the experts working in related areas. As a bonus, it will also help me to collaborate with the attendees in other projects related to my field.
