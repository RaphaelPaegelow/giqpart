My research focuses on the classification of four-dimensional Koszul Artin-Schelter regular algebras.
We determine which families form components of the moduli stack A4 using the Kodaira-Spencer
map. Given a family of algebras A over a base scheme B and point p ∈ B, the Kodaira-Spencer map
gives the derivative of the natural map from the tangent space TpB of the base at p to Hochschild
cohomology group HH2(Ap)0. We also approached the classification problem from the perspective
of superpotentials, applying Schur-Weyl duality and the Borel-Weil theorem to extract discrete
projective invariants that distinguish these components. I am motivated to attend this workshop
to explore moduli spaces of vector bundles and quiver varieties, and to share and discuss my work
with experts in the field.
