I’m Anna Sicolo, PhD student from SISSA. My primary research background lies in deformation
theory, specifically focusing on the formal study of moduli problems and their infinitesimal
structures. I am currently transitioning toward the geometry of Nakajima quiver varieties and
the language of algebraic stacks to better understand the global properties of these moduli
spaces. I aim to apply my experience in deformation invariants to the study of instantons
and their quiver descriptions. I am particularly interested in exploring how the stack-theoretic
approach clarifies the construction of tautological bundles over these varieties.
