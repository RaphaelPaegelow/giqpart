I am working under the supervision of Tony Pantev on understanding the P=W phenomenon and the Kontsevich–Soibelman program for nonlinear Riemann–Hilbert correspondence for log Calabi–Yau surfaces and cluster algebras, together with their noncommutative deformations. I am also working under the supervision of Angela Gibney to study the representation theory of vertex algebras and the sheaves they define on moduli spaces of curves. The summer school’s emphasis on quivers, instantons, and moduli spaces fits closely with my interests in wall-crossing, cluster geometry, and scattering diagram techniques in the sense of Kontsevich–Soibelman, Gross–Pandharipande–Siebert and Gross–Hacking–Keel-Kontsevich. It also connects with my work on vertex algebras through Joyce’s vertex algebras and their interpretation within intrinsic Donaldson–Thomas theory, highlighting a direct overlap between wall-crossing, enumerative geometry, and representation theory.
