1 Research Topic

My current research interest is the Fulton–MacPherson compactification FMn(C2), with a partic-
ular focus on describing its torus fixed locus. Understanding this fixed locus is expected to clarify
the geometry of the compactification.

2 Motivation

My motivation is driven both by a general curiosity for new mathematics and by the Mini-Lecture
series of Prof. Taro Kimura, which may provide a new perspective on the Hilbert scheme of points
in the affine plane.
