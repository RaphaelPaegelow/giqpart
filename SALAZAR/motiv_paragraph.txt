As a mathematician, my research interests are related to algebraic geometry, symplectic geometry and the studying of the structure of the objects in the last one (studying Fukaya Categories, Mirror Symmetry and its role in pure mathematics and mathematical physics) . On the other hand, I am particularly interested as a physicist the display of this tools in physics and how they can help us to understand different dualities that appears in String Theory or how this mathematics could help us to understand the Gauge Theory and QFT as well as the understanding of this objects by themself. This Summer School is a great place to deeply learn about the instantons, enumerative geometry, quivers and the display between pure mathematics and mathematical physics in a stimulating environment.
