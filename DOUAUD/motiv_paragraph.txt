I am currently working on the resurgence of topological string theory on Calabi–Yau manifolds and its connections to Donaldson–Thomas invariants and their wall-crossing behavior, a theory closely related to quivers and extensively studied by Bousseau.
In local cases, topological string theory can also be approached via the Nekrasov partition function, which is the subject of Kimura’s course and a topic I am eager to understand more rigorously.
Overall, this school will provide me with new insights and rigorous geometric tools that will be directly applicable to my research.
