My research is about counting curves (Gromov-Witten theory, K-theoretic Gromov-Witten theory, Cohomological Field Theory, etc). Two of my current projects are potentially related to instantons and quivers. First, I study curve-counts of symplectic quiver variety. They are predicted to be equivalent to curve-counts on certain « dual » symplectic varieties by the 3D mirror symmetry picture from physics. Second, I study genus-zero curve-counts of Hilbert schemes of points on surfaces and instanton moduli. Such counts should be determined by the genus-zero counts.
