My recent research is on the GIT of a specific quiver variety, and more broadly
I am interested in moduli of sheaves and vector bundles. I aim to pivot to applying this area of
algebraic geometry to mathematical physics, given my undergraduate training in quantum field theory.
In particular, I am interested in how objects I know from geometry (e.g. the quotient construction of the
Hilbert scheme of points in C2) relate to physics to inform my future research and thesis directions
