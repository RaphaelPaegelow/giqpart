I am a Master’s student at the University of Bonn specializing in algebraic
geometry and its intersections with representation theory. My current research
interests focus on the geometry of moduli spaces, and I am particularly drawn
to how quiver varieties provide explicit models for these objects. By attending
this conference in Lille, I expect to gain the technical tools necessary to bridge
the gap between abstract moduli problems and concrete quiver descriptions, as
well as having a clearer idea on the possibilities and state of art for my master
thesis.
