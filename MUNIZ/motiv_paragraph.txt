I am interested in the computation of physical observables and geometric invariants in quantum field theory. For instance, the Nekrasov partition function and supersymmetric indices on various geometries, and their relation to holographic dual descriptions, especially in toric settings. In particular, I'm interested on the applications of equivariant localization through instanton calculus, where computations often reduce to the geometric understanding of instanton moduli spaces and of the fixed points of a toric action.
