I am currently working on my Master's thesis titled “Moduli of Quiver Representations and Tautological Bundles.” The focus of the thesis is to study the existence of semiorthogonal decompositions in derived categories of coherent sheaves on moduli spaces of semistable representations of certain framed quivers. In particular, I study categorical actions defined on the derived category of the moduli stack, where representation-theoretic techniques can be applied, and transfer these computations to the semistable locus using “magic window” techniques. I am thus interested in attending this school in order to gain further perspectives on quiver moduli and moduli theory more broadly. I would also welcome the opportunity to learn about the mathematical physics viewpoint on these topics, an area which is yet unknown to me.
