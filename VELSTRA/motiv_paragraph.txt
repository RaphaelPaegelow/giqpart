I work on Gromov-Witten theory and Cohomological Field Theories in general, whose integrals
over moduli spaces of curves produce invariants. In my PhD thesis, I work on several refinements of
these theories by using moduli spaces of spin curves instead. Some open problems include finding
the corresponding refinements for other moduli spaces, including DT theory and the GW theory of
Hilb(C2) through the Nakajima description.
Thank you for your considerations!
