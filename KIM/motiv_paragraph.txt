My research is in algebraic geometry, with a particular focus on surfaces of general type and related some questions. Although my current work is not centered directly on instantons or quivers, I understand that these provide powerful tools for studying vector bundles, sheaves, and moduli spaces on algebraic surfaces, especially in connection with surfaces of general type.  I am highly motivated to attend this workshop in order to learn these methods in a systematic way and to understand how they can be applied to problems concerning surfaces of general type.
