I am a PhD student in Lyon 1 (Institut Camille Jordan) working on algebraic and geometric structures arising from configuration spaces, with an emphasis on Poisson and higher algebra structures motivated by mathematical physics. My research focuses on equivariant algebra bundles and tensor structures related to configuration-space methods appearing in field theory and renormalization. I am particularly interested in interactions between algebraic geometry and geometric structures coming from physics. Attending this workshop would allow me to learn about recent developments in these areas and connect my work with current research directions.
