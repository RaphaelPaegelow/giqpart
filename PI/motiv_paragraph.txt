My research concerns moduli of 1-dimensional sheaves on del Pezzo
surfaces, which admits quiver descriptions and deep connections to
enumerative geometry. One of my papers (arXiv:2211.06991) uses the
instanton moduli space and Nekrasovs partition function as key
ingredients. Other tools in my research such as Virasoro constraints and
cohomological integrality have natural formulations for quiver moduli
spaces. Thus I wish to attend this workshop to learn more about this area.
