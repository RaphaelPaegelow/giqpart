My research focuses on the study of moduli spaces, particularly those of vector bundles, Higgs bundles, and quiver bundles/representations. I am especially interested in moduli-theoretic problems that admit analytic interpretations via partial differential equations, such as moduli spaces of instantons. So far, my work has mainly concentrated on moduli spaces of quiver representations and quiver bundles, where I have obtained results that I would be very interested in presenting during the school. More recently, I have also begun exploring connections between instanton sheaves, quivers, and their derived categories.

I believe I would greatly benefit from participating in this event, as its themes closely align with my current research interests. It would also provide an excellent opportunity to interact with experts in the field and potentially foster future scientific collaborations.
