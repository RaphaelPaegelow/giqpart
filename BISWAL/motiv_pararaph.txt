My research concerns combinatorial representation theory, particularly the study of characters of Demazure modules and Weyl modules for current and affine Lie algebras. Foundational work of Ryosuke Kodera and Kiyoshi Naoi relates these modules to geometric structures arising from quiver varieties. The themes of Geometry of Instantons and Quivers 2026 therefore lie very close to the broader context of my research. Participation in this workshop would help me better understand the geometric frameworks underlying these combinatorial phenomena and foster interactions with researchers working on quiver varieties and related areas.
